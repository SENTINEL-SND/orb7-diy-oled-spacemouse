• Lower Values (e.g., 0.5 - 0.8): Higher sensitivity → FASTER movement.

2. Modifier Curves & Slopes (Q8)

📐 HOW MODIFIER CURVES WORK
• Parameter A (Slope at Zero): Flattens small inputs near rest position for ultra-precise micro-adjustments without accidental jitter.
• Parameter B (Slope at End): (Used in Curve 3) Exponentially boosts response near max deflection for rapid 3D camera panning.
(Curve 3 Tangent is highly recommended for CAD & 3D Modeling)

1. Thermal Drift Compensation Engine

💡 THERMAL DRIFT ENGINE GUIDE
• Sample Accumulation Points (COMP_NR): Number of stable reads averaged to compute baseline offset.
• Monitoring Wait Time (COMP_WAIT): Idle delay before locking a new thermal zero point.
• Noise (MDIFF) & Center (CDIFF) Limits: Safety gates preventing active physical movements from being mistaken for thermal drift.

2. Exclusive Mode Tuning

💡 EXCLUSIVE HYSTERESIS GUIDE
• Switch Hysteresis: Force barrier required to break mid-motion dominance lock between Translation and Rotation. Prevents unwanted mode flickering.

💡 SENSOR ALIGNMENT GUIDE
• Differential Pairs (N, S, E, W): Opposing Hall Effect sensors measuring physical magnetic balance.
• Delta (Δ): Absolute difference between paired sensors. A low delta (Δ ≤ 50) confirms proper magnet centering and housing balance.
• High Delta (Δ > 50): Indicates mechanical tilt or uneven bolt tension requiring physical adjustment.

4. Signals & Thermal Drift Offsets

💡 SIGNALS & THERMAL DRIFT GUIDE
• Thermal Drift: Center point wandering caused by MCU ambient heating, thermal expansion, or mechanical spring hysteresis.
• Raw Read (0 - 1023): Direct unfiltered ADC voltage output from physical Hall sensors.
• Centered Read: Normalized deviation from rest position (Raw - Center + Offset).
• Thermal Drift Offset: Dynamic correction factor continuously computed by the MCU to prevent resting cursor drift.
