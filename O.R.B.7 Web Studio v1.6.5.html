1. Individual Axis Sensitivities (Q7)

💡 SENSITIVITY ADJUSTMENT GUIDE (Q7 DIVIDERS)
In the C++ fixed-point engine, sensitivity parameters act as mathematical dividers.
• ▲ Increase Value (e.g., 0.6 → 2.5): Decreases sensitivity. Axis moves SLOWER and requires larger physical displacement.
• ▼ Decrease Value (e.g., 2.0 → 0.5): Increases sensitivity. Axis moves FASTER with light hand effort.
(Use asymmetric Z settings to balance stiff TPU push-down vs light pull-up forces)

2. Axis Inversions & Swapping

🧭 STANDARD CAD DIRECTION & ORIENTATION GUIDE
• ON (Inverted): Reverses hardware signal direction. Reverses viewport camera movement.
• OFF (Normal): Preserves natural physical sensor polarity.
• Standard 3DConnexion Industry Defaults (SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Inventor):
  – Pan Left/Right (TX): Normal (OFF)
  – Pan Up/Down (TY): Inverted (ON)
  – Zoom In/Out (TZ): Inverted (ON)
  – Tilt Front/Back (RX): Normal (OFF)
  – Tilt Left/Right (RY): Inverted (ON)
  – Twist Left/Right (RZ): Inverted (ON)

3. Modifier Curves & Slopes (Q8)

📐 MODIFIER CURVE SLOPES ADJUSTMENT GUIDE
• Parameter A (Slope at Zero / Exponent 'a'):
  – ▲ Increase (e.g., 1.15 → 2.5): Flattens central response curve. Expands precision zone for millimeter micro-adjustments.
  – ▼ Decrease (e.g., 1.15 → 0.5): Makes initial movement immediate, sharp, and aggressive.
• Parameter B (Slope at End / Factor 'b' - Mode 3):
  – ▲ Increase (e.g., 1.35 → 1.55): Exponentially accelerates camera velocity near full deflection for rapid panning.
  – ▼ Decrease (e.g., 1.35 → 1.05): Makes end-of-travel acceleration smooth and linear.

1. Noise Gates (Cross-Coupling Mute)

💡 NOISE GATES & HARD CUTOFF GUIDE
• ▲ Increase Gate Value: Suppresses larger parasitic forces from neighboring axes. Prevents accidental rotation while panning, requiring more intentional push force to activate that axis.
• ▼ Decrease Gate Value: Allows ultra-light hand pressure to activate the axis immediately, but may allow accidental axis bleed (cross-coupling).
• MicroGate (Pan X/Y): Dedicated post-matrix low-level noise gate (3 to 10). Mutes 3D-print spring residual bleed while keeping Pan X/Y fluid.
• Gate Negative Z (Gate NTZ): Prevents accidental Zoom Out when panning X/Y.

2. Thermal Drift Compensation Engine

💡 THERMAL DRIFT ENGINE PARAMETER GUIDE
• COMP_NR (Sample Accumulation Points):
  – ▲ Increase (e.g., 50 → 200): Larger averaging window. Prevents brief hand pauses from triggering false drift recalibration, but takes longer to lock new zero.
  – ▼ Decrease (e.g., 50 → 10): Locks new thermal zero point rapidly, but risks mistaking steady hand holding for thermal drift.
• COMP_WAIT (Monitoring Wait Time):
  – ▲ Increase (e.g., 100ms → 500ms): Requires longer continuous rest before starting drift tracking.
  – ▼ Decrease (e.g., 100ms → 20ms): Recalibrates zero almost instantly after releasing knob.

3. Exclusive Mode Tuning

💡 EXCLUSIVE HYSTERESIS PARAMETER GUIDE
• Switch Hysteresis Threshold: Force barrier required to break mid-motion dominance lock between Translation and Rotation.
  – ▲ Increase (e.g., 60 → 200): Requires significantly more intentional force to switch modes mid-motion. Prevents mode flickering for heavy hands.
  – ▼ Decrease (e.g., 60 → 15): Makes switching dominance between Translation and Rotation effortless with subtle hand pressure changes.

⚙️ How "Gates & Drift" Engines Interact With Your Mouse

🛡️ THE 4-TIER FIRMWARE FILTERING FIREWALL
The parameters on this page form a 4-tier filtering firewall inside the ATmega32U4 C++ kinematic engine:

1. Tier 1 - Hardware Oversampling & Integer EMA (ADC Noise Elimination):
Hardware analog sampling (2x oversampling + fixed EMA shift) eliminates electrical ADC noise instantly without floating-point math latency.

2. Tier 2 - Thermal Drift Engine (MCU Temperature Stabilization):
Continuously tracks resting signals and recalibrates zero dynamically as the MCU warms up during extended CAD sessions.

3. Tier 3 - Noise Gates & MicroGate (Mechanical Bleed Elimination):
Strips post-matrix velocity bleed (e.g. 1 to 5) caused by TPU spring micro-asymmetries, ensuring Pan X/Y and Zoom Z remain independent.

4. Tier 4 - Exclusive Mode (Single-Mode Dominance Lock):
Evaluates force dominance and locks the output exclusively to Translation OR Rotation until force drops below the relaxation threshold.
